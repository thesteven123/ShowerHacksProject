# How to take your Tiny Menaces photos

We need **two photos** from you. Send both at full resolution (HEIC or JPEG is fine). **You do not need to crop anything** — we handle that on our side.

## Before you shoot (both photos)

- Use a **plain background** if you can (wall, door, sheet). Avoid busy patterns.
- **Even light** on your face and body — stand facing a window or soft indoor light. Avoid strong backlight (window behind you).
- **No filters**, beauty mode, or portrait blur.
- Wear the **same outfit** in both photos when possible.
- **Solo photo** — just you in the frame.

---

## Photo A — Face (for your gremlin head)

**Goal:** A clear shot of your face for a small in-game portrait.

1. Hold the phone at **eye level**, about **arm’s length** away (or use the **rear camera + timer** to avoid selfie distortion).
2. Frame your **head and a little hair** — not your chest, not a full-body shot.
3. Look at the camera with a **neutral or slight smile**.
4. Fit your face inside the **oval guide** (tall egg shape, not a circle — like an ID headshot matte, **not** iPhone Portrait mode):

   ![Face oval guide](../packages/character-creator/assets/guides/face-oval-overlay.png)

   - Forehead inside the top of the oval  
   - Chin inside the bottom  
   - Face centered  
   - Leave a little space above your hair and below your chin  

5. Keep **light in front of your face**, not behind you.

**Avoid:** Hats that hide your hairline, heavy shadow on one side, tilted head, sunglasses.

---

## Photo B — Full body (for arms, legs, and torso)

**Goal:** One photo from head to feet so we can build your chibi body.

1. Step back until your **whole body** is in frame — small gap **above your head** and **below your feet**.
2. Stand **feet shoulder-width apart**, weight even.
3. **Arms straight down**, slightly away from your sides — about a **hand’s width** between each arm and your body.
4. **Hands relaxed**, open or lightly toward your legs — **not** in pockets, not crossed, not on hips.
5. Face the camera **square-on** (shoulders parallel to the camera).
6. **Same clothes** as Photo A when you can. Avoid long coats or wide sleeves that hide your arms and legs.

**Avoid:** Sitting, legs crossed, one foot stepped way forward, one arm hidden behind you, cropping toes or the top of your head.

---

## Don’ts (both photos)

- Group shots or other people in frame  
- Sunglasses or face coverings (unless that’s always how you look — tell us)  
- Extreme angles (photo from above/below)  
- Dark room or strong colored lights  

---

## How to send

- **Two files:** face photo + full-body photo  
- **Highest resolution** your phone allows  
- Any filenames are fine — text us who the photos are for  

That’s it. Thanks for being a Tiny Menace.
